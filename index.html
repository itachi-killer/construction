Engineering consultancy and career education under one roof

Consultancy. In buildings we offer architectural and construction services: detailed design, structural analysis, MEP design, BIM, sustainability advice, site analysis, code compliance and construction inspection. In water and waste water engineering we work on the whole water chain: water supply and treatment plants, sewerage and sewage treatment plants, faecal sludge treatment, restoration of lakes and rivers, feasibility reports, detailed project reports and project management services.

Career education for fresh graduates. Our education wing helps new engineers become job-ready. We offer classroom and online training in the software used every day on engineering projects: AutoCAD, WaterGEMS, GIS (Google Earth, ArcGIS, QGIS) and MS Office tools such as Excel and Word. Courses are taught by experienced professionals, built around hands-on projects, and finish with certification and career support.

Whether you are a client with a project to plan or a graduate looking for a strong start in engineering, we would be glad to hear from you.

Buildings

Every building project is carried from first concept to completion: detailed design, structural analysis and construction management, with safety, cost and schedule kept in view.

  • Structural engineering design and analysis
  • MEP design: HVAC, electrical and plumbing
  • Project management and BIM
  • Sustainability consulting and energy efficiency
  • Site analysis, feasibility studies and code compliance
  • Construction inspection and quality assurance

Water and Waste Water Engineering

We work on the water chain as a whole: supplying safe drinking water, collecting and treating wastewater, and restoring water bodies for public and private clients.

  • Water supply networks and treatment plant (WTP) design
  • Sewerage, sewage (STP) and faecal sludge (FSTP) treatment design
  • Lake and river restoration studies
  • Feasibility reports and detailed project reports (DPR)
  • Project management (PMU, PDMC, PMC)
  • Construction supervision, quality control and third-party inspection
